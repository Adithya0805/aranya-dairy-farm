# Project Roadmap — Aranya Organic Dairy Farm

**Client**: Aranya Organic Dairy Farm, Shoolagiri, Hosur, Tamil Nadu  
**Project Code**: ARY-2026  
**Status**: Phase 1 — Frontend Setup  

---

## 1. Project Overview & Vision
Aranya Organic Dairy Farm is a 9-year established organic farm in Shoolagiri, Hosur, Tamil Nadu.
Phase 1 focuses on creating a premium, trust-building, mobile-first frontend site to showcase their pure A2 milk, ghee, and organic practices.

## 3. Task ARY-P1-03 Details — Site Structure Requirements
The Next.js frontend application must implement the following key sections and components:

1. **Header & Navigation**:
   - Logo with organic leaf badge.
   - Quick jump links: Home, About, Products, Process/Gallery, Reviews, Contact.
   - Direct Call / WhatsApp CTA action button.

2. **Hero Section**:
   - Powerful headline celebrating 9+ years of pure organic dairy farming in Shoolagiri, Hosur.
   - Trust highlights (100% Raw A2 Milk, Grass-Fed Cows, Zero Preservatives).
   - Primary CTAs: "Explore Products" & "Order via WhatsApp".

3. **About & Story Section (9-Year Legacy)**:
   - Story of Aranya Farm established in 2017 in Shoolagiri.
   - Organic grass feeding, chemical-free pastures, ethical cow care, and zero antibiotics.
   - Interactive hygiene process timeline/cards (Milking → Testing → Chilling → Doorstep Delivery).

4. **Products Catalog Section (Phase 1 Frontend View)**:
   - Organic A2 Raw Whole Milk (1L / 500ml glass bottle display)
   - Traditional Bilona Cow Ghee
   - Fresh Farm Cottage Cheese (Paneer)
   - Pure Set Curd & Butter
   - Highlighting nutritional benefits, eco-packaging, and price tags.

5. **Gallery & Farm Life Section**:
   - Visual showcase of free-roaming cows, green pastures, modern hygiene equipment, and fresh delivery.

6. **Testimonials & Social Proof Section**:
   - Highlighting 3.6★ rating on Justdial (15+ reviews) and verified local customer testimonials from Hosur & Bengaluru.

7. **Contact & Location Map Section**:
   - Farm Address: Shoolagiri, Hosur, Tamil Nadu.
   - Direct Phone, Email, and Google Maps embed location card.
   - Direct inquiry form (Phase 1 frontend state).

8. **Sticky Floating WhatsApp Button**:
   - Fixed bottom-right action widget for instant customer connection.
